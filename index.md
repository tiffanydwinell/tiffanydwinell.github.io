[NOAA Buoys Temperature Trend Project (1999-2025)](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1W8xehiSRHrlF6nvNKGjg3Aytl6Ex2pMR?usp=sharing)
<b>Summary:</b>
In this project, I identified a significant ocean warming trend (+0.049°C/year) after using regression to control for geographical and seasonal variation. However, the geographic distribution of buoys was an important confounding factor, where buoys shifted toward warmer locations over time. When restricting the analysis to buoys observed consistently over time, the estimated warming trend decreased by 38%.

[SCCOOS Pier Pressure Time Series Analysis (2021)](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1IOU2oAcNATKU3J9i5EVK3XYG6jAwpJIL?usp=sharing)
<b>Summary:</b>
In this project, I used least-squares fitting and Fourier analysis to model automated pressure data from the Scripps Pier in 2021. To apply Fourier analysis effectively, I selected a time period within 2021 with fewer anomalous measurement increments and linearly interpolated missing values. As a result, I identified three major tidal constituents in the data, which were confirmed by plotting the spectrum as well.
